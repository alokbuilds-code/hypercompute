CHAPTER 03 / THE FOUNDERS & 29,600 BUILDERS

CULTURE OF REINVENTION.

April 5, 1993 • Denny's Diner, San Jose

Where Accelerated Computing Began

NVIDIA was conceived at a roadside Denny's booth in San Jose, California, where Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem met over endless coffee. Their radical hypothesis: PC graphics would evolve from 2D spreadsheets to immersive 3D simulations requiring dedicated, highly parallel silicon coprocessors.
